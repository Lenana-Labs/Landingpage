Most problems arrive labelled as technology problems. Half of them are questions about how work actually happens. We start there — with the process, the constraint, the person at the end of it — and build outward.

01 / About

Curiosity meets engineering.

We are an early-stage studio, which is a constraint and an advantage: fewer layers, more attention, and the freedom to try something properly before committing to it.

LenanaLabs works in three directions at once. Client work sharpens the craft. Internal products let us live with our own decisions. Research keeps the assumptions honest. Based in Nairobi, working with teams wherever they are.

03 / Approach

Four stages. One habit: check the assumption.

  1. 01 — Understand

    Read the situation

    The process, the constraint, the people who will actually use it. Before proposing anything.

  2. 02 — Experiment

    Buy information cheaply

    Build the smallest thing that could disprove the idea. Find the hard part early, while it is still small.

  3. 03 — Engineer

    Build it properly

    Clear interfaces, tests where they matter, no cleverness the next person cannot maintain.

  4. 04 — Refine

    Remove what did not earn it

    Watch it work. Measure what changed. Take out whatever turned out to be decoration.

Stages overlap. We do not pretend otherwise.
